Figures:
- must be referenced, preferably before they are shown
- caption must be speicific enough to be able to stand alone
- if not your figure => source: ... at the end of the caption OR adepted from: ...

Tables:
- must be referenced in text
- clear caption, capitalized
- do not clutter with grid lines
- reference if not yours
- Text left alligned
- numbers right alligned or at the decimal seperator
- column with symbols can be center alligned but prefferably left alligned

Symbols:
- 1 letter symbols are written in italic
- in math mode they are automatically written in italic => force them back upright
- same goes for symbols in subscripts
- symbols for quantities are upright

thounsands seperator:
- \num{} to keep the number together

only write significant numbers

Equations:
- should be part of the sentence => use punctuation
- multiplication use \cdot or \times

citation:
- \cite
- \parencite
- \textcite
- \citeauthor
